## 2. Guarantee and operator contract

A successful API call establishes availability for one request at one time. It does not establish zero price, an account plan or protection against future charges. An empty balance or missing payment card alone is not a universal no-charge control. Catalog/list prices may be positive while a genuinely free account has included access; such prices alone neither disqualify an asserted account nor certify its effective cost.

Account-mode opt-in asserts that the **actual billing path of this credential** cannot incur operator-paid inference: free/included promotional access only, paid overflow/PAYG/postpaid/auto-recharge disabled, no purchased credit balance being consumed, and no third-party billing/BYOK/organization bill-to path. Exhausted free allowance must reject rather than continue into paid usage. For HF this explicitly includes account-side provider preferences/BYOK, which the ordinary inference key does not prove absent. This assertion is the operator's responsibility, not a machine-verified observation. A false or stale assertion can incur charges, including the first probe; the guard cannot undo them. Included gratis allowance may be consumed and is not a zero model-rate claim.

Keep OpenRouter strict and disallow selecting its account mode. Its exact approved source/auth/inference binding, fresh prices, exactly `["text"]` output modalities, unsupported-fee rejection and owned `provider.max_price` prompt/completion/request/image string `"0"` plus `require_parameters=true` remain mandatory. Never retry without those constraints or reinterpret omitted request pricing as explicit zero.

In account mode `state=free` means **conditionally eligible under the operator assertion**, with `basis=user_confirmed_account_probe`, `assurance=user_asserted`, `reason=account_asserted_available`. It must never be presented as provider-certified zero price. Keep strict OpenRouter bases unchanged and annotate diagnostics with `assurance=provider_enforced`. The existing `/v1/models` schema and `auto:free` alias stay compatible; opting in allows this explicitly documented mixed-assurance pool. Operator documentation and safe CLI diagnostics must explain the distinction and count routes by assurance. No new public API/UI is introduced. Cost unavailable remains unavailable, never zero or synthetic savings.

## 5. Private qualification and evidence lifecycle

Run an asynchronous worker under the existing discovery lifecycle; listing and source refresh do not wait for inference. Catalog candidates start unknown/pending, excluded from ordinary selection. An internal `ProbePermit` authorizes one exact pending route, assertion/scope epoch, request profile, mode, deadline and budget reservation. It is never a public eligibility record, cannot be supplied by clients and cannot call normal fallback. Reuse the owned request validator, HTTP adapter and BillingGuard without temporarily setting registry `is_free`, bypassing universal guard hooks or fabricating positive evidence.

One qualification is a buffered request and an SSE request to the **same route and scope**, each `max_tokens=32`, fixed non-sensitive prompt `1+1=?`, no tools/add-ons/provider options. Each must return actual HTTP 200 with a valid completed text response and non-whitespace visible content; SSE must have valid terminal completion and exactly one DONE. Reported completion usage, if present, must not exceed 32. Empty/null/role-only/reasoning-only, unexpected tool-only output, malformed/truncated/error/media output or missing SSE terminal is not a pass. A length finish with no visible text is inconclusive, not proof of incompatibility, paid pricing or future success. No prompt adaptation/model-specific workaround is introduced. Both modes must pass before publishing an availability lease. No public request telemetry is created by these internal probes.

The pair is invalid if a scope/capability/route change, assertion revocation, source expiry or hold occurs before publication. Recheck after every awaited stage. Positive/invalid recognized operator charge publishes the shared hold before any further send/publication. Complete probe content stays internal and does not override the hold; do not send the second mode after an incident. Concurrent pending/active requests observe the same scope hold. Shutdown/cancel closes responses/clients, releases permits and awaits worker tasks; no dangling retry or late publication.

Keep two independent clocks: source freshness (existing defaults above) and availability lease (24h from the completed pair, renewal due after 12h). Admission requires both, plus assertion/current epochs and no blocking hold. A catalog refresh, queue wait or another route's success cannot extend a lease. Removed/incompatible routes are revoked immediately; expiry removes admission even with a congested queue. A source failure removes admission immediately but may retain an unexpired private lease cache for reuse only after fresh identical route/capability/scope data returns. Capability change requires requalification. Expired/failed leases never become free through cache reuse. Any failed qualification or renewal pair immediately revokes that route's previous availability lease and private pass cache, even if its old TTL has not expired; retain no old-pass admission while retrying. Failures do not certify a model as paid. A negative result applies to this exact route/scope, except credential/account-wide outcomes defined below.

Keep statuses/reasons finite and safe: assertion required, credential missing/revoked, pending/probing, available, expired, source unavailable, incompatible, probe inconclusive, retryable and held. Diagnostics include basis/assurance, source/probe ages, remaining lease and safe hold/reason; no prompt, answer, raw error body, secret substring or account identity. Public model-list calls remain memory-only and initiate no probes or catalog I/O.

## 6. Resource budgets, fairness and failures

Use fixed tested bounds for this version, not additional operator tuning knobs:

- One pair per provider credential, two pairs globally; each HTTP request at most 15s end-to-end, pair at most 30s; decoded response at most 4 MiB (or existing stricter adapter bound). A timeout/cancel closes owned resources.
- Sliding-minute limits: at most four probe HTTP sends globally, two per provider. Sliding-24h limits: 192 globally, 96 per provider. Every attempted send, including timeout/error/cancel/retry, consumes budget; reserve atomically immediately before send. A rejected reservation sends nothing. Credential change does not reset process budgets.
- Of the global 24h budget, reserve 64 sends for new-candidate pairs and 128 for renewals; no borrowing in this version. Initial qualifications and retries without a prior valid lease use the new-candidate partition. Renewals use the renewal partition. Both still obey the shared provider/global limits; second-mode reservation must fit remaining pair deadline, otherwise abandon the pair without admission. Never publish a one-mode pass.
- Maximum five simultaneously available route leases per provider, selected dynamically. At most 1,000 queued distinct routes per provider, coalesced by exact identity; fair provider rotation and candidate cursor, with bounded renewal priority within its partition. No manual model preference/ID seed. Continue fair new-candidate exploration within its partition even while the active subset is full. A fresh successful new pair atomically replaces the oldest published active lease in that provider when all five slots are occupied; failure never evicts a healthy incumbent. Do not evict before the replacement pair passes. Cached-source recovery may restore a still-valid pass only into a vacant slot and never exceed five; it cannot replace a fresh incumbent or resurrect a failed pass. Do not requalify a healthy active route before renewal is due merely to consume exploration budget.

At capacity, twenty active routes renewed twice daily require eighty successful HTTP sends per day, below the 128-send renewal partition; the remainder permits bounded failures, not a promise of continuous availability. New-candidate capacity is thirty-two successful pairs per day across all four providers before failures. Real catalog size, quotas and failures can prevent qualification of every route. Promise fair gradual exploration and a fresh bounded active subset, never simultaneous validation of an unbounded catalog. All counters/queues/leases remain in memory; restart resets local budgets and leases, so this is not a durable cross-restart quota firewall. Document that limitation.

429 pauses the provider probe queue using bounded Retry-After (1..300s, default 60s); 5xx/network/inconclusive responses retry after fixed 60s with normal budget/fair rotation. 400/unsupported protocol gives a one-hour negative result or earlier invalidation on relevant source change; 403 is a candidate access failure unless an existing documented classifier identifies an account hold. 403 without a documented account hold gives a one-hour route-negative result, invalidated earlier by relevant source change. 401 invalidates all availability leases for that credential and suspends its probes until deliberate scope redeclaration/restart. Retrying a failed pair after its fixed delay requires a complete new pair and the new-candidate partition; it cannot reuse its earlier one-mode success. None proves paid pricing. No adaptive/exponential T-009 breaker is added.

Reuse existing documented 402/balance classification and scope hold. Do not invent substring classifiers or silently reinterpret all 402 as paid. **No automatic balance-recovery probe:** any observed balance hold, whether created by a real consumer request or a qualification, suspends that credential's automatic probes for the rest of the process; the worker never acquires a balance-recovery permit after expiry; an explicit operator retry/restart may try after the transient hold expires. A previously valid unexpired route can still use existing next-real-user-request bounded balance recovery; automatic probes never clear the suspension or hold. A latched financial incident always dominates transient recovery. No new admin retry endpoint is introduced; an isolated CLI retry is explicit operator action and obeys that process's budgets/holds. A separately started CLI has its own in-memory counters and guard, cannot inspect or certify clearance of a primary-process hold, and must not be reported as primary balance recovery. Cross-process financial coordination/durable budgets are outside this contract.

## 11. Source interpretation and unresolved limits

The [RECON](../docs/recon/T-007-remaining-provider-eligibility.md) is the measured source of catalog counts and their dates; no fixed count/model ID is an acceptance condition. [Groq billing](https://console.groq.com/docs/billing-faqs) separates free/paid access; [spend limits](https://console.groq.com/docs/spend-limits) have delayed accounting. [NVIDIA hosted access](https://docs.api.nvidia.com/nim/docs/run-anywhere) does not itself provide the missing key-scoped enforcement contract. [Mistral keys](https://docs.mistral.ai/admin/identity-access/api-keys) inherit organization settings and [billing](https://docs.mistral.ai/admin/billing-usage/billing) warns about processing lag. [HF pricing](https://huggingface.co/docs/inference-providers/main/pricing) presently gives no included Free-user credits; [Hub API](https://huggingface.co/docs/inference-providers/main/hub-api) exposes per-provider observations, and [provider selection](https://huggingface.co/docs/inference-providers/index) documents exact selectors. These sources justify separating discovery/availability from account trust, not claiming successful probes determine pricing.

Account-side billing changes invisible to the normal key are not automatically detected. Short qualification prompts do not prove quality, all future parameter combinations, large context, quota durability or absence of charges. Catalog/probe TTL bounds availability staleness, not assertion truth. Strict cost-proof support for other providers, additional gateways, local/cloud provenance, adaptive recovery and frontend assurance controls remain separate work. No account browser scraping, guessed billing endpoint, legal guarantee or hardcoded credit/RPM entitlement is introduced.
